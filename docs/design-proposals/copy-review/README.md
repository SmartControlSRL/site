# Copy review — 8 September 2026

The owner asked for language that sounds less inflated and less like generated
marketing copy. The revision covers Home, four service pages and two product
pages in Romanian and British English, including their descriptions, related
service cards, contact sections and shared footer.

Headings now name the service or activity. Paragraphs describe the work, with
fewer abstract promises and fewer repeated references to coordination. The
combined-service delivery model remains clear for clients with several IT needs.

## Reference sites

- [Kyndryl — Technology services](https://www.kyndryl.com/us/en/services): the
  opening service description uses concrete verbs to explain the work across
  the life of an IT system.
- [Bechtle — IT services](https://www.bechtle.com/gb/it-services): practical
  service examples explain what an external provider can take on for an IT team.
- [NTT DATA — Managed Network Services](https://services.global.ntt/en-us/services-and-products/enterprise-networking/managed-network-services): the
  individual service descriptions name the network environments and the work
  involved in managing them.

These passages informed the tone. Their claims, scale, service commitments and
product capabilities are not evidence for Smart Control. All three also contain
more promotional language; the reference is their concrete service descriptions.

For example, “O perspectivă comună asupra infrastructurii, evenimentelor și
riscurilor” becomes “Monitorizează infrastructura și centralizează evenimentele
pentru echipele de operațiuni și securitate.” Managed service names are shorter
and easier to scan, such as “Administrarea rețelei” / “Network management”.

## Review scope

The approved brand tagline, action labels and email routing remain in place.
Contractual service levels and applicable source-code terms retain their scope.
No quantitative claim, certification or new product feature was introduced.

The existing Open Graph images and their registered hashes remain unchanged.
Automatic approval review rejected re-registering rewritten social images
without commercial/legal approval, so that separate change was excluded.
