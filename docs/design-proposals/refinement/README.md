Reviewed changes: shorter service heroes, removal of repeated hero lists and
homepage process strip, balanced product grids, clearer RO/EN copy, consistent
product demo actions and independently generated background images. Each service
has a distinct hero image, following the owner's correction to the initial shared
service artwork. Software and Managed Services show their entire sculptures;
Infrastructure and Security retain an architectural and macro composition.
The product headers also have distinct images: a signal collector for SEKNET and
a protected connection for S-VPN. Their conceptual summaries sit beneath the main
hero content instead of covering the image.
